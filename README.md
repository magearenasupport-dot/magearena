# Privacy Policy for Mage Arena

**Effective Date:** October 28, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 28, 2026  

This Privacy Policy describes how **Mage Arena** ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you download, install, and play the mobile game **Mage Arena** (the "Game") on Android devices.

By installing or playing Mage Arena, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

#### A. Information You Provide Directly
Mage Arena does **not** require any account registration, login credentials, usernames, passwords, or personal profile details. We do not directly collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.

#### B. Local Game Data (On-Device Storage)
All gameplay progress, character levels, unlocked magic schools, Hall of Fame records, audio/haptic preferences, and run statistics are stored **strictly locally on your device** (via local storage). This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to our own servers.

#### C. Information Collected Automatically by Third Parties (Advertisements)
Mage Arena integrates **Google AdMob** (provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited) to serve optional rewarded video advertisements (e.g., revives and power-ups). When ads are requested or displayed, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process certain device and usage data, including:
- **Device and Advertising Identifiers:** Google Play Advertising ID (AAID) and unique device identifiers.
- **Network and Technical Information:** IP address (used for approximate coarse location and fraud prevention), device model, operating system version, carrier, and screen resolution.
- **Ad Interaction Data:** Views, clicks, impressions, and performance diagnostics.

For more information on how Google collects and processes ad data, please review Google's Privacy Policy and Terms:
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

---

### 2. How We Use Information
We and our advertising partner (Google AdMob) use the collected information solely to:
- Serve rewarded video advertisements and deliver in-game rewards.
- Display relevant and personalized ads (where consent has been granted).
- Detect and prevent fraudulent clicks and invalid traffic.
- Analyze ad delivery performance and diagnose technical crashes.

---

### 3. Consent and User Choices (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

#### A. European Economic Area (EEA) and UK Users (GDPR)
In compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the ePrivacy Directive, we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to present a consent dialog before serving personalized ads. 
- You have the right to grant, refuse, or withdraw consent at any time.
- You can change your ad consent preferences inside the Game by navigating to: **Options (⚙) > Privacy Options (Ad Consent)**.

#### B. California and US State Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)
We do not sell your personal information in the traditional sense. However, the use of third-party advertising identifiers may be considered "sharing" or "selling" under California law. 
- You can opt out of interest-based ads directly through your Android device settings by navigating to:  
  `Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID` or `Opt out of Ads Personalization`.

---

### 4. Data Retention and Deletion
- **Local Data:** You can permanently delete all local game data at any time by uninstalling the Game or clearing the app storage via your Android device settings (`Settings > Apps > Mage Arena > Storage > Clear Data`).
- **Ad Identifiers:** Advertising data collected by Google is retained according to Google's standard retention schedules. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID via Android system settings at any time.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)
Mage Arena is designed for general audiences aged **13 and older** (or 16 and older in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal data through our third-party services, please contact us immediately, and we will take steps to ensure appropriate deletion.

---

### 6. Security
We value your trust in providing us with your information. Because Mage Arena operates locally without custom backend servers, your gameplay data remains on your physical device. Advertising network communications are transmitted securely using industry-standard HTTPS encryption.

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
